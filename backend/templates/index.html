How it works
  1. 01Capture a road-surface image (camera / dashcam / phone).
  2. 02OpenCV isolates dark, irregular surface regions.
  3. 03A RandomForest classifier verifies true potholes vs. shadows/cracks/markings.
  4. 04Detections are severity-ranked and geo-tagged.
  5. 05Results are logged for the maintenance dashboard.
